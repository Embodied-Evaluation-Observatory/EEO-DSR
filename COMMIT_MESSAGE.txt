EEO-DSR V0.1: entry points by material, open licensing, and the dataset-side review module

One record per corpus, one reading (NEES), with interval, cap and the unestimable boundary;
five dimensions and six card checks underneath. This is the starting release; the version series
begins at V0; V0.1 adds the entry and licensing layer.

Thresholds and measurement
- tau is derived, not picked: nearest-neighbour pairing bounds matching error at half a period
  (0.5), zero-order hold at a full period (1.0). Records state pairing_policy, tau_factor,
  pairing_direction and period_basis. The period is the sampled stream's (camera frames paired to
  nearest state samples), not a free choice; an unknown direction takes the shortest period as the
  strict end. One global tau is a simplification and the record says so; per-pair tau is the ideal.
- W is bound to its metric: family embedding_cosine, statistic cosine_similarity, plus
  threshold_binding. Cosine 0.95 is not perceptual-hash 0.95, so W without a metric is a bare
  constant.
- The dedup detector carries the same known-truth gate as the clock model: at least 20 known
  duplicate and 20 known distinct pairs, false merge and split rates at most 0.05, passed true, and
  a runner that exists. A gate that cannot run is not a gate.
- The default package records acceptance_use not_declared_by_buyer, prints that it may not serve as
  an acceptance finding, and that the accepting party must declare thresholds and rerun.

Counting
- The pool is pass + unverified; degraded carries zero weight and never enters NEES.
- Structural faults are typed; an identified fault never enters unverified. unverified means only
  that timing cannot be measured; a detected break that cannot be classified routes to
  alignment_unlocatable.
- A spike that crosses a boundary with no internal break leaves a trace in
  stream_continuity_findings (count plus episode boundaries). It does not enter NEES or any
  dimension reading; it exists so a third party can recheck why the effect was zero.
- The chain prints four fields: sync_filtered, sync_unverified, dedup_count, nees; null rather than
  omission, and sync_unverified may not exceed sync_filtered.
- Dedup count is the union of metric-and-W duplicates and byte-identical copies, without double
  subtraction.

Scope
- This version does not assess label consistency and does not run D1b (cards versus an index).
  Both are declared in scope_declaration with the reason and when they return, rather than dropped
  silently. D1-D5b are local codes, not a crosswalk with earlier materials.
- Diversity supports one decision (whether to ask for more practice variation within a task) and
  explicitly does not support whether the corpus trains a better policy. Multi-task records are
  stratified; no bare pooled value.

Verification
- tools/validate_record.py: 138 self-test cases; tools/run_all_checks.py runs the self-test, all
  seven example records, the observation and a JSON Schema cross-check.
- tools/dedup_calibration.py: deterministic known-truth calibration at the declared W.
- checks/README.md prints EEO-RN-3: screening is arithmetic on cards, not a conformity check.

Entry points and licensing
- Three gates by material, not identity: public cards (free screen, no login or permission),
  commissioned sample (paid pre-purchase check; thresholds declaration, sampling frame and
  DATA_TERMS first), full corpus (deep verification; plus data agreement and compute).
  Commissioned records are public and name the commissioning party; certification, rating and
  endorsement have no gate at all.
- Interfaces carry no score, rating, ranking or adjective; a FAIL cell shows the declared and
  recomputed values; rows sort by letter or date, never by failure count; aggregates say only
  "n of m cards fail at least one check"; every page prints the use limitation before any number.
- Code is MIT, this module's computed output is CC-BY-4.0, third-party fields stay under source
  terms. Method, schema, validator, pure arithmetic, screening script, clock model and dedup
  detector with their known-truth fixtures, and the observation format are open. Corpora, samples,
  buyer-private documents, unpublished drafts and quotes are not.
- Paid work is a dated, reproducible, named record on the client's sample at declared thresholds,
  not code. Fork defence is verifiable, not legal: a stripped record fails the official schema and
  validator, citations require method_version, corpus id and version, date and record hash, and
  the name and record-format identity are not licensed.

Implementation order is set by falsifiability: card screening first, then the dataset-side driver
with pure arithmetic, then cluster bootstrap, clock model, dedup and diversity.
