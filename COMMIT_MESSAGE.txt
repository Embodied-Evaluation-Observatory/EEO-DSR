EEO-DSR V0.2: dataset-side review spec, validator, and first Phase 0 screen

This release ships the method, the record and observation schemas, the validator, the threshold
declaration, and the first dated card screen. It does not ship any NEES reading of any corpus and
does not state whether any corpus is usable.

Dedup gate
- Known-truth calibration needs at least 59 known duplicate and 59 known distinct pairs per class,
  not 20: 0/20 has a 95% upper bound of 13.9%; 0/59 is 4.95%.
- False merge and false split are reported separately, each with its point estimate and exact
  Clopper-Pearson 95% upper bound. They move NEES in opposite directions and are not pooled.
- A record carries executed, fixture_hash and result_hash; the runner file must exist, must have
  executed, and must have passed. Existence alone is not a gate.

Metric identity
- The dedup metric family is one enum without synonyms: embedding_cosine, perceptual_hash,
  exact_byte.
- W is bound through an object {metric, w, calibration_fixture_id}; the bound metric must equal the
  family, the bound W must equal thresholds.dedup.w, and the fixture id must equal the calibration
  fixture id.

Counting
- The chain is an equation: sync_filtered = n_pass + n_unverified, and sync_unverified <=
  sync_filtered.
- Stream continuity findings stay out of the NEES formula, but every buyer summary carries one line
  with the count, so the finding is visible to a summary reader.
- global_tau_note states that the global tau takes the strict end and that this can only lower
  NEES. The global tau remains a simplification; per-pair tau is the ideal.

Scope
- Dimension codes carry the DSR- prefix: DSR-D1 through DSR-D5b. They are a namespace, not a
  crosswalk with earlier materials.
- Label consistency and D1b are still declared as not done, with the condition for their return.

Phase 0
- checks/phase0_screen.py fetches 12 public Hugging Face LeRobot cards that need no login or
  approval, records each raw README sha256, and checks S1 to S6.
- Result dated 2026-11-25: 12 of 12 cards fail at least one check. S1, S2 and S4 pass on all 12;
  S3, S5 and S6 are MISSING on all 12. This is card arithmetic, not a verification finding.

Verification
- tools/validate_record.py: 147 self-test cases.
- tools/run_all_checks.py: self-test, seven example records, the observation example and a JSON
  Schema cross-check.
- tools/dedup_calibration.py: 59 known duplicate and 59 known distinct pairs, zero false merges and
  zero false splits, upper bound 0.049508 per class.
- checks/phase0_screen.py: deterministic on repeat runs for the same date.

Release boundary
- Published files are listed in RELEASE.md and hashed in SHA256SUMS.
- Corrections ship as a new version; no silent edits, and old tags remain.
